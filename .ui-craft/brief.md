# Project Brief

<!-- Always-loaded by the ui-craft skill. Keep this concise — it anchors every design decision. -->

## Product Identity
**SAKIP (Sistem Analisis & Konsolidasi Kinerja Instansi Pemerintah)** — A modern performance accountability and cascading tree platform for government institutions (Kementerian / Lembaga / Pemda) adhering to PermenPAN-RB standards.

## Design Intent
**Modern Minimal SaaS** — Sleek low-contrast borders, generous whitespace, subtle micro-interactions, and Geist/Linear-style clean aesthetics. Replace cluttered bureaucratic government templates with a calm, high-precision productivity workspace.

## Audience
1. **Perencana Kinerja (Planners)**: Constructing cascading strategic trees, formulating outcomes, outputs, and performance indicators.
2. **Reviewers / Asesor**: Auditing cause-and-effect cascading logic, verifying indicator quality, and issuing formal review decisions.
3. **Pimpinan Instansi**: Reviewing executive achievement summaries and institutional progress.

## Voice and Tone
- **Precise & Objective**: Clear labeling using standard government performance nomenclature (Sasaran Strategis, Outcome, Output, Indikator Kinerja Utama).
- **Transparent AI Assistance**: AI recommendations clearly highlighted with step-by-step reasoning and explicit accept/dismiss controls.
- **Calm & Focused**: No loud badges, no rainbow status pills, no generic gradients.

## Constraints & Core Knobs
- **CRAFT_LEVEL**: 8 (High precision, structured component contracts)
- **VISUAL_DENSITY**: 6 (Balanced SaaS spacing with high data readability)
- **MOTION_INTENSITY**: 5 (Smooth transitions for drawers, tabs, and canvas zoom; respects `prefers-reduced-motion`)
- **ACCENT_COLOR**: Deep Emerald / Teal (`#0F766E` / `#059669`) — represents target achievement, governance, and institutional clarity. 90%+ neutral palette.
